# TypingManiaNovel 20260726

TypingManiaNovel 20260726 turns the original QQ Music workflow into a common,
privacy-conscious local library pipeline for QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music,
Apple Music, and user-selected folders.

## Highlights

- Choose 1–500 songs in game (10 by default), stop safely with `Esc` or
  `Backspace`, keep completed work, and review the latest five failures.
- Import ordinary downloads and validated complete cache media; verify song
  identity, timed lyrics, language-specific pronunciation, direct production,
  original work title, poster, and album cover before packaging.
- Refresh trusted metadata without overwriting it with an ambiguous lookup;
  reset one source, edit multiple songs, or review same-provider duplicate
  suggestions.
- Optionally show a locally cached MV. Playback is off by default. A
  recording- and timeline-verified MV is preferred; verified official footage
  from the direct production may loop as a visual-only fallback. Accepted
  files are normalized to browser-safe H.264 MP4, stored under a stable song
  identity, and reused without redownloading. An unavailable or failed MV
  falls back to the verified poster and switches MV playback off to prevent
  repeated lookups.
- Stop a song-info refresh immediately with `Esc` or `Backspace` while keeping
  completed atomic updates.
- Use region-aware, health-adjusting source priorities and bounded timeouts in
  mainland China, Hong Kong/Macau, Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, Southeast Asia,
  the United States, Europe, and other regions.
- Verify the production that directly used a song across animation, film,
  television, documentaries, commercials, variety shows, sports events,
  visual novels, JRPGs, and games. Unverified original titles and artwork stay
  blank.
- Keep TypingMania NEO scoring and rank thresholds for comparable scores while
  adding richer low-latency Keyfall feedback and distinct starter melodies.
- Read shortcuts directly from compact menu buttons, use neutral song markers,
  and keep pending lyrics clear of the former translucent spectrum layer.
- Import both QQ Music MFLAC and MGG caches, recover exact metadata through
  lyric-assisted media-ID matching when needed, and continue past tracks the
  signed-in account cannot access.

## Install

Download `TypingManiaNovel-20260726-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and
double-click `start-game.cmd`. Node.js, npm, Python, FFmpeg, and importer setup
are not required. A new launch replaces this copy's previous service and
opens a dedicated Edge game window with a `1920 × 1080` game viewport by
default; its title bar and frame sit outside that area. Use `terminate.cmd` to
close both without touching unrelated processes.

The public archive contains only the three generated starter songs. Imported
audio, lyrics, artwork, MVs, account/session data, music-client caches, logs,
and local-library state are excluded.
